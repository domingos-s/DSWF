🔥 Simmer Down

Use DSWF before the fight happens.

When you feel yourself getting heated, tap I’m getting heated. Simmer Down starts an auditory distraction, gives you a short cooling exercise, helps you name the trigger, and asks you to choose the response you want to be proud of later.

🔥Notice it earlyChoose who you’re getting heated with before the argument has momentum.
🧯Break the patternAudio plus paced breathing creates an immediate interruption before you respond.
🚒Choose your moveSay it calmly, take 10 minutes, let it go, or assume good intent. DSWF can even give you words to use.
🕊️Come back laterWhen you’re ready, return to DSWF and record whether you cooled down, stayed tense without fighting, or fought anyway.
No nagging notifications. The check-in is intentionally user-led. Returning to finish the flow is part of the reflection.
How it works

A fuller picture of family life.

DSWF tracks conflict without making conflict the center of the relationship. Peace streaks, recognition, interventions, reflection, badges, and insights all live together.

01

Build relationship profiles

Every family member gets a profile with their photo, current and best streaks, positive recognitions, and a dated badge history.

02

Notice the good stuff

Give recognition when someone listens, helps, shows kindness, exercises self-control, persists, or makes a good choice. Add an optional note so you remember why.

03

Reward rehabilitation

Each recognition adds +2 Peace Score points, capped at 100, so positive behavior can help rebuild standing after conflict rather than making a bad moment permanent.

04

Keep the streak going

Each relationship has its own real-time peace streak and milestone ladder. A fight resets the clock, not the relationship.

05

Interrupt the spiral

Use Simmer Down before a fight to interrupt the moment, cool off, identify the trigger, choose a response, and later record what happened.

06

Reflect and learn

Guided journaling, experiments, and on-device Insights help surface recurring triggers, timing, emotions, repair patterns, and which interventions appear to help.

Recognition badges

Celebrate patterns, not perfection

Positive recognition unlocks profile badges at meaningful milestones—starting with the first good thing you log and continuing as the positive record grows. Peace-streak badges are also attributed to the individual relationship where they were earned.

Private intelligence

Insights that get better with use

DSWF looks for patterns in your own records: common triggers, difficult times of day, emotion patterns, changing streak lengths, relationship-specific trends, and whether certain Simmer Down strategies are associated with better outcomes. Optional 7-day experiments turn those insights into something you can actually test.

Install the PWA

Use it like an app.

Open DSWF in your phone’s browser once, then add it to your home screen. It launches in its own app window and supports offline use.

Android / Chrome

  1. Open DSWF in Chrome.
  2. Tap Chrome’s ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Confirm the installation. DSWF will appear with your other apps.

iPhone / Safari

  1. Open DSWF in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Launch DSWF from the new home-screen icon.
Private by design

Your family data isn’t the product.

DSWF does not require an account or backend. Names, photos, recognitions, profile history, streaks, fights, Simmer Down sessions, outcomes, and journal entries remain in your browser’s local storage unless you explicitly export a backup. Journal access can also be protected with a 4-digit PIN inside DSWF.
