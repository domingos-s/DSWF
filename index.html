How it works

Simple enough to use. Useful enough to learn from.

DSWF starts as a streak tracker and quietly becomes a private relationship journal and pattern finder as you use it.

01

Add your people

Create family members with a name and optional profile photo. Each relationship gets its own independent streak.

02

Keep the streak going

The clock counts real 24-hour periods. When a quarrel happens, tap We fought and that relationship starts fresh.

03

Reflect, don't scorekeep

A guided journal helps you process what happened, what you felt, what triggered it, and what you want to do differently.

04

See the history

Your reflection registry and calendar keep a private record of difficult days and the streaks that came before them.

05

Spot patterns

After enough observations, the on-device Insights Engine can surface recurring timing, trigger, emotion, streak, and repair patterns.

06

Keep it private

Names, photos, fights, streaks, and journal entries stay in your browser's local storage. DSWF has no account or backend.

Install the PWA

Use it like an app.

Open DSWF in your phone's browser once, then add it to your home screen. It launches in its own app window and supports offline use.

Android / Chrome

  1. Open DSWF in Chrome.
  2. Tap Chrome's ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Confirm the installation. DSWF will appear with your other apps.

iPhone / Safari

  1. Open DSWF in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Launch DSWF from the new home-screen icon.
Private by design

Your family data isn't the product.

DSWF does not require an account and does not send your relationship journal to a server. Your data stays on the device unless you explicitly export a backup.
